# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Codingsols to be a welcoming place for programmers of every background and experience level. We pledge to make participation in this project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners.
- Give constructive feedback on code, not on people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and assume good intent.
- Credit others' work and ideas.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Spam, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem harmful.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers by opening a [private security advisory](https://github.com/nevil-codes/codingsols/security/advisories/new) or by contacting the repository owner on GitHub. Reports will be handled confidentially.
